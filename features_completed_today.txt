================================================================================
                    NEXACE CRM - FEATURES COMPLETED TODAY
================================================================================

1. HR PARTNER ASSIGNMENT & ONBOARDING ROUTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dynamic HR Assignment: Employees can be assigned a dedicated HR Partner
  (hrId / hrName) during registration or profile management.
- Intelligent Fallback to Workspace Admin: If no dedicated HR Partner is
  assigned, onboarding document review, checklist management, and approvals
  automatically route to the Workspace Admin.
- Onboarding Document Submission & Review Hub: When new hires submit compliance,
  identity, or NDA documents, the assigned HR Partner (or Admin fallback)
  receives instantaneous in-app alerts and notifications with quick review links.
- Enterprise HR Data Isolation: When 'isolateHRData' is enabled, HR Partners only
  access and manage onboarding checklists, documents, timesheets, and invoices
  for their assigned employees, while Admins retain tenant-wide visibility.

2. DUAL-PORTAL TIMESHEET & INVOICE REVIEW & APPROVAL WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dual Portal Access: Review and approval actions are accessible across:
  a) HR Portal (/dashboard/hr) under the dedicated "Timesheet & Invoice Approvals" tab.
  b) Admin Invoices / Finance Portal (/dashboard/settings?tab=invoices & /dashboard/finance).
- Multi-Role Support: Both HR Partners (role="HR") and Workspace Admins have the
  authority to review, approve, or reject employee invoices and timesheets.

3. MANDATORY APPROVAL GATE BEFORE PAYMENT PROCESSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Strict Backend Enforcement: /api/it/invoices/[id] and /api/finance/invoices/[id]
  strictly reject payment requests (status="Paid") with HTTP 400 Bad Request
  unless the invoice status is explicitly "Approved".
- UI Guard Rails: "Process Payment" action is completely locked and hidden for
  pending or unapproved invoices. It becomes available only once an HR Partner or
  Admin formally approves the submission.
- Separation of Approval & Payout: Review/Approval and Disbursement/Payment are
  cleanly separated into two distinct stages in the workflow.

4. AUTOMATIC LINKED TIMESHEET SYNCHRONIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- One-Click Auto-Approval: Approving an IT contractor or employee invoice
  automatically transitions all associated pending timesheet records (TimeEntry)
  for that billing period to "Approved", eliminating double-entry overhead.
- Audit Stamping: Records approver identity (approvedBy), timestamp (approvedAt),
  and organizational role (approverRole: "HR" | "Admin" | "OPS").

5. FORMAL REJECTION SYSTEM WITH ACTIONABLE REASONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Mandatory Rejection Reason: Rejection requires a mandatory explanation modal
  detailing what needs correction.
- Employee Self-Service Feedback: Rejected invoices prominently display the
  rejection reason banner and reviewer details on employee self-service cards and
  list views, guiding the user to revise their timesheet and resubmit.

6. UI / UX & DESIGN SYSTEM ENHANCEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- FontAwesome 6 Icon Compliance: Standardized across the approval and invoice
  workflows using FA6 classes (fa-check-double, fa-ban, fa-file-invoice-dollar).
- Mobile-First Responsiveness: All dialogs, status badges, action toolbars, and
  feedback banners adapt seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
